Our ground rules

NO TEAM JERSEYS.

Editorial independence

We do not campaign for or against political parties. Every party receives the same profile structure and the same evidentiary standards. Sponsored political content is not disguised as journalism.

How we classify claims

Convicted: a court conviction. Charged: formal charge or prosecution. Under investigation: an official investigation. Allegation: a claim not established as fact. Acquitted: found not guilty or acquitted.

Sources and corrections

We prioritise IEC records, court documents, municipal records, original manifestos and attributable statements. Every factual item should show a source, date and update history. Corrections must be visible, not quietly buried.

Right of reply

People and parties named in material allegations should be offered a fair opportunity to respond. Their response, or the fact that no response was received by publication, should be recorded.

This is an independent information project, not an IEC publication and not voting advice. Election dates and participation details must be confirmed against current IEC notices. Projections must be labelled with their source, fieldwork dates and methodology.
